About ROFARIM

ROFARIM Pharmaceutical Company Limited is a proposed pharmaceutical and biomedical research initiative based in Bangladesh.

Our vision is to contribute to scientific advancement through innovative research, molecular medicine, hormonal science and next-generation biomedical technologies.

We aim to build a research-driven organization dedicated to exploring complex biological systems and future medical discoveries.
